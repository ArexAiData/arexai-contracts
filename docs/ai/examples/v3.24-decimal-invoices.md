This completes the fixture introduced in PR #48. Its `v3.24` filename is retained
for continuity; it is not the current product version. The rates are illustrative
test inputs in synthetic monetary units. No country, tax period, exchange rate
or legal applicability is asserted.

## Row meaning and decisions

Each data row is one invoice line. Physical CSV row 1 is the header. `quantity`
is units, `unit_price` is monetary units per unit, and the two percentage columns
are rates divided by 100. IDs are exact text. Customer labels are synthetic. The duplicate-review scenario explicitly requires one ID per unique invoice; the CSV alone does not establish whether repeated IDs represent multiple invoice lines.

INV-005 occurs twice at physical rows 6 and 7. INV-006 at row 8 has the price
`INVALID`. Neither row removal nor a zero-price substitution is implicit. The
approved example explicitly selects rows **2–6**, excludes the second occurrence
at row 7 and excludes row 8. These decisions are illustrative user approvals,
not a general deduplication policy; the full seven-record source is preserved.

## Three reproducible questions

1. **Which records need review before an unconditional total?** INV-005 has two
   occurrences, and INV-006 has an invalid price. Ask one unresolved business
   decision at a time. Do not invent a whole-file total.
2. **For explicitly approved rows 2–6, what are net, tax and gross?** Net **54.22**,
   tax **10.84**, gross **65.06**. These totals do not apply to all seven records.
3. **How do the boundary records behave?** Row 4 has a 100% discount and zero net,
   tax and gross. Row 5 has zero quantity and zero amounts. At row 6, tax is
   `0.05 × 10% = 0.005`, rounded per line with ROUND_HALF_UP to **0.01**; gross is
   **0.06**. Applying a different rounding policy would change this result.

For every approved row, calculate base = quantity × price; discount =
ROUND_HALF_UP(base × discount rate, 2); net = base − discount; tax =
ROUND_HALF_UP(net × tax rate, 2); gross = net + tax. Row 2 therefore has base
59.97, discount 6.00, net 53.97, tax 10.79 and gross 64.76. Row 3 contributes
0.20 / 0.04 / 0.24. Rows 4 and 5 contribute zero; row 6 contributes
0.05 / 0.01 / 0.06.
